Tips

Show it. Describe it. Fix it.

Fixing what went wrong is called debugging. You do not have to find the cause yourself. Give the AI good evidence and let it do the work.

How to debug

Take a screenshot

Paste it straight into the chat. Windows: Win + Shift + S. Mac: Cmd + Shift + 4.

Copy the exact error

If you see an error message, paste all of it. Do not retype or summarize it.

Ask the AI to fix it

Say what you did, what you expected, and what happened. Ask for the complete corrected file.

Ask the AI to explain it

“Explain what went wrong in plain words.” You learn a little every time.

Debug prompt

Something is not working. What I did: [clicked the Submit button on my phone]. What I expected: [a thank-you message]. What happened instead: [nothing]. I attached a screenshot and pasted my full code below. Find the cause, explain it in plain words, then give me the complete corrected index.html.

Be more ambitious

Most people ask AI for too little. Asking costs nothing, and the worst outcome is that you learn where the limit is.

  • Think creatively

    Ask “What else could this do?” and let the AI suggest ideas you would not have thought of.

  • Connect your spreadsheet

    Paste a few sample rows and ask for a page that charts or searches them.

  • Use software you already have

    Name the tools in your day, such as email or a calendar, and ask what could be built around them.

  • Try it and test its limits

    Ask for the ambitious version first. If it fails, scale back one step.
