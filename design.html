Design

Look around. Sketch it. Then build.

Anyone can vibecode an app, so design and user experience (UX) are what set yours apart. Skip them and you get AI slop (opens in a new tab) or, worse, a slop grenade (opens in a new tab).

AI slop & slop grenade

01 AI slop

AI-generated content that is produced quickly and published without much human thought, taste or editing. You can usually tell at a glance.

02 Slop grenade

Unreviewed AI output handed to someone else, who then has to spend their own time checking and fixing it.

Examples

In writing

  1. Filler openers such as “In today's fast-paced world”
  2. Overused words such as “honestly” and “actually”
  3. Everything listed in colons and bullet points
  4. Many words, very little said

In design

  1. The same purple-to-blue gradient on everything
  2. Emoji used as icons in every heading
  3. Generic headlines and stock-looking AI images
  4. Overuse of cards and boxes

Mock it up before you ask AI to build

A mockup is a rough picture of what you want. It takes ten minutes and saves an hour of back‑and‑forth, because the AI can see what you mean.

  1. Collect references

    Find two or three pages you like and note what you like about each: the colors, the fonts, the layout.

    See the list of inspiration sites ↓

  2. Sketch the layout

    Draw boxes for the header, the main content and the buttons. Sometimes pen and paper is enough.

    See tools for mocking up ↓

  3. Choose a few colors and fonts

    Two or three colors and one or two fonts. Fewer choices look more deliberate.

  4. Show the AI

    Attach a screenshot or photo of your mockup and your references, then ask it to build what it sees. Copy this prompt and change the words in [brackets]:

    Design prompt

    Build this page to match the attached mockup. Use the attached reference only for style. Colors: [navy, cream, one orange accent]. Fonts: [one for headings, one for body text]. Keep the spacing consistent and the text easy to read. Do not add sections I did not draw. Put everything in a single index.html.
