Design

Look around. Sketch it. Then build.

AI can build almost anything you describe. What it cannot do is decide what is worth building or how it should feel. That part is yours.

Why design matters more now

  • Building is no longer the hard part

    When anyone can produce a working page in minutes, the difference is in the choices: what to include, what to leave out, and how it looks.

  • AI defaults to average

    Without direction, AI gives you the most common version of everything. That is why so many AI-made pages look alike.

  • People judge in seconds

    Visitors decide whether to trust a page almost immediately. Clear, careful design earns that trust.

  • Your taste is the input

    The more clearly you can show what you want, the better the result. A reference or a sketch beats a long description.

Definition

AI slop

Low-effort AI-generated content that is produced quickly and published without much human thought, taste or editing. You can usually tell at a glance.

In writing

  • Filler openers such as “In today's fast-paced world”
  • Overused words such as “delve” and “seamless”
  • Everything listed in threes
  • Many words, very little said

In design

  • The same purple-to-blue gradient on everything
  • Every piece of content inside a card
  • Emoji used as icons in every heading
  • Generic headlines and stock-looking AI images
  • Uneven spacing and text that is hard to read

Mock it up before you ask AI to build

A mockup is a rough picture of what you want. It takes ten minutes and saves an hour of back-and-forth, because the AI can see your intent.

  1. 01

    Collect references

    Find two or three pages you like from the inspiration sites below. Note what you like about each: the colors, the fonts, the layout.

  2. 02

    Sketch the layout

    Draw boxes for the header, the main content and the buttons. Pen and paper is enough.

  3. 03

    Choose a few colors and fonts

    Two or three colors and one or two fonts. Fewer choices look more deliberate.

  4. 04

    Show the AI

    Attach a screenshot or photo of your mockup and your references, then ask it to build what it sees.

Design prompt

Build this page to match the attached mockup. Use the attached reference only for style. Colors: [navy, cream, one orange accent]. Fonts: [one for headings, one for body text]. Keep the spacing consistent and the text easy to read. Do not add sections I did not draw. Put everything in a single index.html.

Tools for mocking up

  • Figma (opens in a new tab)The standard design tool. Draw screens and link them into a clickable prototype.
  • Canva (opens in a new tab)Drag-and-drop layouts. The easiest place to start if you are new to design.
  • Paper (opens in a new tab)A design canvas, also listed under Tools.
  • Slides or pen and paperA slide, a whiteboard photo or a napkin sketch works too.
